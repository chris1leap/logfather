Master heartbeat gaps

Only the master's heartbeat on 700 is checked. A gap is the time from one heartbeat to the next; the red marks on the slider show where they are. The monitor keeps only part of the traffic, so a gap here may be the monitor dropping frames rather than the device going quiet; the count of frames the monitor saw in that second is shown to help judge.
